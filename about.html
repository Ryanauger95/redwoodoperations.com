About Us

Built to Operate

We bring institutional-quality management to every property in our portfolio, combining deep operational expertise with an owner's mentality.

Redwood Operations LLC is a full-service commercial property management and acquisitions company. We specialize in managing and improving commercial buildings, delivering institutional-quality oversight for assets of all sizes.

Our team brings deep operational expertise to every property we manage. From tenant relations and lease administration to capital planning and facility maintenance, we treat every asset as if it were our own — because in many cases, it is.

We combine hands-on management with a disciplined approach to acquisitions, identifying commercial properties where our operational capabilities can unlock value. Our focus spans self storage facilities and multi-tenant industrial buildings across the Southeast.

Based in Greenville, South Carolina, we operate with the conviction that great property management starts with being present, responsive, and accountable. Every decision we make is grounded in protecting and growing the long-term value of the assets we oversee.

Meet the Founder

Ryan Auger

Founder & Principal

Ryan Auger founded Redwood Operations to bring institutional-quality management to commercial real estate assets in the Southeast. With a hands-on approach and an owner-operator mindset, Ryan oversees every aspect of the portfolio — from acquisitions and lease negotiations to capital improvements and day-to-day facility operations.

Ryan built the Redwood platform from the ground up, starting with self storage facilities and expanding into multi-tenant industrial properties across Georgia and South Carolina. His approach combines disciplined underwriting with a deep commitment to operational excellence, ensuring that every property performs at its highest potential.

Based in Greenville, South Carolina, Ryan is actively involved in the local business community and brings a founder's intensity to every property under management.
