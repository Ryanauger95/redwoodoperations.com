Privacy Policy

How Redwood Operations handles information on this website and in the apps our team uses.

Last updated November 22, 2026

Who we are

Redwood Operations LLC ("Redwood," "we," "us"), based in Greenville, South Carolina, runs this website and the business software our team uses at its subdomains, including the Redwood CRM (crm.redwoodoperations.com) and Redwood Mail (mail.redwoodoperations.com). These apps are for our own team and our affiliated companies: only people we give an account can sign in. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

This website

redwoodoperations.com has no forms, analytics, advertising or cookies. If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads; Google's own privacy policy covers that.

What our apps collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and what our single sign-on needs to sign you in.
  • Business records our team enters, such as properties, deals, contacts, notes and files.
  • Email from a mailbox you choose to link, as described below.

Linking a Google account

A team member can link their own Google account to the Redwood CRM or to Redwood Mail. We access Google user data only after you allow it on Google's consent screen, and only for the features below.

Redwood CRM: sending email from deals

  • Your name and email address (openid, email, profile): to show which mailbox is linked.
  • Send email (gmail.send): to send the emails you write on a deal from your own mailbox. The CRM sends only emails you write and choose to send.
  • Read email (gmail.readonly): to find replies in conversations you started from the CRM and copy them onto that deal. The CRM checks which new messages belong to those conversations and downloads only those; it does not open or keep your other mail. If you have no signature set in Gmail, it reads your most recent sent emails to work out your signature and keeps only the signature.
  • Basic Gmail settings (gmail.settings.basic): to import your Gmail signature. The CRM only reads it.

Redwood Mail: an email app with AI-written drafts

  • Read, send and organize email (gmail.modify): to show and search your mail, send the emails you write, save drafts, and archive, label, snooze or set reminders when you ask.
  • Basic Gmail settings (gmail.settings.basic): to use your signature.
  • Google Drive, read only (drive.readonly): to find and read files that help answer an email, and to attach a file to a reply. Redwood Mail never changes or deletes your files.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.readonly): to show your calendar, suggest times you're free, and accept or decline invitations when you ask.
  • AI drafts: to prepare draft replies and answer questions you ask about your mail, Redwood Mail sends the email thread, plus any files, calendar times and CRM records it looks up, to Anthropic's Claude model through Amazon Bedrock. Under Amazon's terms, Amazon Bedrock does not store these requests, use them to train models, or share them with the model's maker. Drafts are saved to your Gmail Drafts. The AI never sends email; only you do.
  • Read receipts: when you choose to track an email you send, Redwood Mail adds a tiny image to it and records the time it is opened. Nothing else about the recipient is recorded.

How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features described above for the person who linked the account.

  • We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • We share it only with the service providers that host and run our apps (Amazon Web Services, including Amazon Bedrock for Redwood Mail's drafts), when the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, with notice to you.
  • No one at Redwood reads it, except teammates who can open the deal a reply was copied to in the CRM, someone you ask to help with a specific message, when needed for security (for example, investigating abuse), or to comply with the law.

Redwood Operations' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft 365 mailboxes

Outlook mailboxes linked to the Redwood CRM work the same way through Microsoft Graph: the CRM sends the emails you write and copies replies in conversations you started from the CRM onto that deal.

Where data is stored and how it's protected

  • Our apps run on Amazon Web Services in the United States.
  • The sign-in tokens that let an app reach a linked mailbox are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are never shown to anyone.
  • Our apps require a Redwood sign-in, and all traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Emails copied onto a deal are stored with that deal. Redwood Mail keeps a copy of your mail in our database and in your browser so the app is fast.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can unlink a mailbox at any time: in the Redwood CRM under Deal settings → Email accounts, or in Redwood Mail's Settings. Unlinking deletes the stored sign-in tokens right away, and the app stops reaching your mailbox. Nothing is deleted from your Gmail.
  • You can also remove our access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Emails already copied onto deals stay with those deals as business records. To have them, or Redwood Mail's copy of your mail, deleted from our systems, email info@redwoodoperations.com and we will delete them.

Children

This website and our apps are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
